How it works

You hear the person’s real voice naturally, plus a translated track delivered through bone conduction. When you reply, the companion device speaks your message in their language, so the exchange feels normal on both sides.

1) Listen naturally

Your ears still hear the speaker directly. Ear adds a translated layer, tuned for clarity.

Bone conduction keeps your ear open, reducing isolation and fatigue.
2) Interpret meaning

The model targets intent, tone, idioms, and culture, not literal word matching.

Designed to stay coherent when people interrupt, laugh, or change direction.
3) Speak back

Your reply is voiced by the companion device, so the other person hears it in their language, out loud.

Natural turn taking, no phone passing, no awkward screen reading.

FAQ

Does this do word for word translation? ⌄

No, the goal is meaning. Ear tries to preserve what the person intends, including tone and idioms, so the conversation feels natural.

Why bone conduction? ⌄

Bone conduction lets you hear translation without blocking your ears, so you still hear the speaker’s real voice and the environment naturally.

What about privacy? ⌄

A good product design offers choices, on device processing when feasible, encrypted transit, and clear controls. This website includes a privacy first positioning section you can expand into a full policy.

Is the demo real translation? ⌄

The on page widget is simulated so it runs everywhere with no keys. When you are ready, connect it to a translation API and a text to speech engine, the UI is already structured for that.
