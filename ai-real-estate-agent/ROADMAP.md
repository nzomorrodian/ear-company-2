# Roadmap

## MVP
- Seller intake and profile
- Property details capture
- Pricing engine stub with comp list
- Listing copy draft and checklist
- Showing schedule preferences
- Offer comparison UI
- Closing checklist

## Integrations
- Comps data provider (ATTOM, Estated, MLS)
- Listing syndication + MLS
- Scheduling (Calendly or ShowingTime)
- E-sign for offers and disclosures
- Payment for flat fee checkout

## Later
- AI negotiation assistant for counters
- Document vault and audit trail
- Market activity alerts
- Buyer inquiry triage
